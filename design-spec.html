Signature — "the rising water"

A waterline anchored to the bottom of the screen that physically rises as flood risk grows (see the three phones above: thin line → climbing → risen + red). It visualises Mumbai's real fear — water rising in the street when rain meets tide — instead of the generic falling-rain every weather app uses. It's the one bold element; everything else stays quiet. Motion: slow ambient swell only; prefers-reduced-motion → one static line.
